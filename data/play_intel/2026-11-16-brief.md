## Bottom line

Nothing in this cycle's underlying permit, rig, or lease picture has moved in eleven days — the pipeline's own run log has been silent since 11/05, TX RRC has been blind for 60 days, and even LA's feed, which is actually current through 11/02 at the source, hasn't been re-pulled. That's now three consecutive cycles of a worsening, unaddressed outage and it should be escalated directly rather than noted again next cycle. Underneath that frozen permit book, however, real capital keeps moving: Mitsubishi's $7.5B Haynesville deal is closed and dated (July 14-15), Devon-Coterra ($58B, May 7) and SM-Civitas ($12.8B, January 30) are both closed in the Permian, and Comstock-SOCAR ($1.65B), Crescent-Devon ($4.2B), and Continental-Firebird are all still pending — the last now running roughly two months past its own guided close with zero public confirmation either way. The two positions worth having plat and surface work ready for before any of this clears — Aethon/Adamas's 16-well Clay Creek buildout in Washington County and Firebird's 12-well Ector County campaign — are both fully permitted, multi-week-committed, and still sitting idle 74-137 days out; whenever TX RRC's block lifts or Continental's deal closes, either could move fast with little advance notice.
