## Bottom line

The single most important fact this cycle isn't a deal — it's that the ingestion pipeline itself is now the story. What the 11/09 brief flagged as a 4-day gap has become a full week of zero movement across TX, LA, and the lease feed simultaneously, with no evidence it's being actively worked; this needs a direct fix on the Task Scheduler side, not a third consecutive "still broken" note next cycle. Second: this cycle's most consequential finding is a correction, not a discovery — the Magnolia CEO "crisis" repeated in the last two briefs was a misdated 2022 story, and should be struck from the client-facing record rather than carried forward again; it's a reminder that every recycled claim in this pipeline needs the same scrutiny the fresh ones get, especially when the source is an aggregator page rather than a primary filing. Third, underneath the frozen TX snapshot and the unclosed Comstock-SOCAR and PBT-Blackbeard deals, the one place in this entire dataset showing genuine, current, multi-operator drilling momentum is Bienville Parish, Louisiana — Adamas and Comstock both permitting fresh pads there inside the last three weeks — which is where this cycle's land/GIS attention should actually go while the rest of the board sits frozen.
