## Bottom line

The mechanical pipeline's outage has now stretched past two full weeks with no fix in sight — TX RRC blind for 63 days, the run log silent for 14, and even LA's genuinely current (11/02) source data sitting unpulled since 11/05 — so every permit and lease count in this brief is identical to the 11/16 cycle's, and that repetition is itself the signal: this needs a direct fix to the Task Scheduler jobs, not another cycle of flagging it. What did move this cycle is Diamondback's calendar: a $5B deal to buy out the rest of Double Eagle (closing December 21) and a KKR-backed buyout of the remaining Deep Blue water-infrastructure stake, both announced in the nine days since the last brief — the clearest sign that capital is still actively consolidating the Permian even while this pipeline's own view of it is frozen. Elsewhere, the pending-deal queue barely moved: Comstock-SOCAR picked up a reported (not primary-sourced) November 4 signing date but no confirmed close, and Crescent-Devon, Continental-Firebird, and SoftVest-Blackbeard are all still sitting exactly where they were three cycles ago. The two positions worth having plat and surface work staged for — Aethon/Adamas's 16-well Clay Creek buildout in Washington County and Firebird's 12-well Ector County campaign — are both still fully permitted and idle, 77-140 days out, and with TX RRC's own data frozen, a plat crew showing up without warning in either county is now a real risk this pipeline can't give advance notice of.
