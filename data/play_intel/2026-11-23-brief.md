## Bottom line

The single most important fact this cycle isn't a permit count — it's that two of the three plays just had a top-3-ranked operator by permit volume absorbed into a buyer within the last seven months: Coterra into Devon in the Permian (closed May 2026) and Devon's own Eagle Ford book sold onward to Crescent (announced Oct 2026, same company, opposite direction). Expect operator-of-record names in this evidence to shift under those two deals within the next cycle or two, independent of anything in the ground changing. Layered on top of that, Haynesville's growth is now unambiguously being financed by foreign, LNG-linked capital — Mitsubishi, Tokyo Gas, JERA and reportedly Osaka Gas have all taken direct upstream positions in the play in the last 18 months, with AI-data-center gas demand (Comstock's Anderson County power-hub deal) as the newest concrete driver. None of this is visible in the mechanical RRC/SONRIS pipeline, which remains dark (67 days TX-side, 18 days LA-side) — this cycle's value is entirely in the web-research layer, not the frozen permit counts underneath it, and that gap is worth flagging on its own: whoever owns the always-on PC's Task Scheduler and the RRC relationship should treat this as overdue, not routine.
