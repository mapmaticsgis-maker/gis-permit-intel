## Haynesville (Texas + Louisiana)

Full TX+LA permit history against the standard Haynesville footprint: **123 TX core permits** (Harrison 29, Panola 29, San Augustine 26, Rusk 20, Shelby 10, Nacogdoches 9), **34 more** in the West Haynesville extension (Freestone 9, Robertson 7, Anderson 5, Leon 5, Madison 5, Houston 3), and **143 LA permits** in the tracked "RROG + DOXA — NW Louisiana Haynesville" corridor (De Soto 50, Caddo 38, Red River 17, Bossier 15, Sabine 11, Bienville 5) — **300 combined**. TX core leaders: Sabine Energy Inc. (24), Adamas Energy (12), RFE Operating (10), Rockcliff Energy Operating III (10). LA leaders: Apex Natural Gas (31), Expand Operating (26), Comstock Oil & Gas-LA (15), BPX (12), Adamas (11).

**A data nuance worth flagging up front: the lease file's own play-tagging splits this corridor in a way that matters for how to read it.** Enverus's `DI Play` field tags only Harrison/Nacogdoches/Rusk/Smith/San Augustine/Panola/Gregg/Upshur as "HAYNESVILLE - TX" and Caddo/Red River/Bossier/De Soto/Sabine/Bienville as "HAYNESVILLE - LA." The West Haynesville extension counties (Leon, Madison, Robertson, Freestone, Anderson, Houston) are either tagged "EAGLEBINE" by Enverus (Madison, Robertson, and a fraction of Leon) or carry **no play tag at all** (Freestone, Anderson, Houston, and most of Leon — 2,198 of the 10,537 rows in today's file have a blank `DI Play`). That's a genuine coverage gap in Enverus's own classification for this corridor, not a data-entry error on this pipeline's part — this brief follows `config.yaml`'s "West Haynesville watch" corridor definition (by county) rather than the play tag, consistent with how the trade press treats this zone as deep Haynesville/Bossier-equivalent drilling stacked under a shallower Eaglebine-tagged lease.

With that grouping, **Haynesville (TX core + LA + West extension) accounts for 5,015 of the 10,537 leases in this file — 1.66 million acres across 24 counties/parishes** — the largest play-level slice by a wide margin, concentrated in Leon (994), Harrison (727), Nacogdoches (452), Caddo-LA (414), Madison (360), Rusk (341), Smith (286), and Anderson (276).

**Comstock Resources is both mid-transaction with a major LNG-linked partner and the largest or second-largest lessee in this file, depending how its affiliates are counted.** Comstock and Azerbaijan's SOCAR signed a framework agreement September 28 advancing their $1.65B Haynesville partnership (20% non-op interest in Legacy Haynesville, 15% in Western Haynesville, 15% of Comstock's Pinnacle Gas Services stake); **the definitive purchase-and-sale agreement is still targeted for no later than October 31 — 23 days from this brief — with close by year-end,** and no signing has been reported yet. Separately, Comstock began funding a **$450M drilling joint venture with a Jerry Jones family partnership on September 1** — distinct from the SOCAR deal — covering 85% of costs on 18 Western Haynesville wells and 80% on nine Legacy Haynesville wells over the next 12 months. On the ground: "Comstock O&G Louisiana" alone shows **591 new leases** in this file, almost entirely in Leon and Freestone counties (404 and 165 respectively) — this is very likely the leasehold feeding that new drilling JV, landing in the data in the same window the JV was announced.

**"Bull Run Resources" is the single largest unidentified lessee in this entire file — 627 new leases, nearly all in Leon (336) and Madison (291) counties, directly inside the West Haynesville corridor.** This search could not identify a company by this name with any confirmed connection to Haynesville, Leon, or Madison County (the only "Bull Run" references found are unrelated gas leases in Reeves County, 300+ miles away in the Permian). Flagged rather than guessed at — this is either a landman/title entity acting on behalf of a known operator, or a genuinely new entrant worth a direct name check before the next cycle.

**Apex Natural Gas remains the play's most active driller by every available measure and is also its largest tracked-family lessee this cycle.** Mid-2026 reporting (SynMax) puts Apex at **11 active rigs and 6 frac crews** — more combined activity than any other Haynesville operator — with production up roughly 3x, from ~0.58 Bcf/d (Jan 2025) to ~1.73 Bcf/d (mid-2026); other sources cite anywhere from 7 to 9 rigs, so treat the rig count as a range, not a confirmed October figure. Apex leads LA permits (31) and, in this file, picked up **652–657 new leases** (depending on alias grouping) worth 22,755.9 acres — dominant in Caddo Parish (372 of Caddo's 414 total) and Nacogdoches County (196 of 452). Today's LA digest adds two more Apex permits in Red River Parish (Brewer 33-14-10 HU, wells 003/004) — its third active Red River program per the 10/7 intel-insight read (alongside an already-unspudded 4-well DSP pad and a 131-day-old Kuhn pad).

**Expand Energy is the clearest case of a tracked family whose leasing has moved from its LA base into the TX extension corridor.** Company guidance calls for 11–12 rigs and $2.75–2.95B of 2026 capex, more than half Haynesville-directed. In this file, Expand picked up **494–497 new leases (485,501 acres — the single largest acreage total of any tracked family)**, concentrated in Anderson County (205 of 276) and Houston County (224 of 228) — both West Haynesville extension counties where Expand's name barely registered in prior cycles. It still holds the #2 LA permit spot (26 permits, Caddo/Sabine-centered).

**Sabine Energy and its East Texas affiliate remain the largest standing filer and lessee combination inside the TX core.** Sabine Energy Inc. leads TX Haynesville-core permits outright (24), and "Sabine East Texas Basin LLC" picked up **277 new leases (29,664 acres)** spread across five counties — Harrison (94), Smith (49), Rusk (64), Gregg (46), Upshur (24) — the widest single-family county spread in the core. The 10/7 intel-insight read adds texture here: Sabine's unspudded book is unusually dispersed for one operator (Hill CV A/B in Panola at 509 days — the second-oldest unspudded permit in either state — plus pads in Harrison, Rusk, and a non-sequential Bonner North build where three wells permitted a full month apart).

**Adamas (ex-Aethon, Mitsubishi) continues filing under three names in the same county, and the 10/7 intel-insight pull adds a concrete development signature behind the brand transition.** Mitsubishi's $7.5B Aethon acquisition closed in July; Adamas now produces roughly 2.1 Bcf/d, second only to Expand. Separately, the intel-insight read flags Aethon's **Clay Creek development in Washington County — 16 wells across Clay Creek West/East, all permitted in an 8-day window (Aug 25–Sep 2), all still unspudded** — a batch-development signature reading as a frac-ready program awaiting a completion crew, not trickle permitting. **RFE — not a tracked family, but now a top-5 lessee in the corridor's core — stays on the watch list from last cycle, bigger than before.** "RFE ETX IV" and "RFE Overton LLC" combined for **513 new leases** in this file, led by Harrison (407 of Harrison's 727 total) and Rusk (RFE Overton alone is Rusk's #2 lessee at 99). ## Bottom line

The lease-filing feed just delivered a backlog ten times larger than its previous record — 10,537 records — but the honest read is that it's roughly 3.5 months of recordings (mid-June through early October) landing in one manual export, not a single day's surge; treat every count above as a catch-up snapshot, and get the automated daily lease pull working so this doesn't happen again. Inside that snapshot, the Permian edges out Haynesville for the largest play-level slice (3,255 leases vs. 5,015 if the full West Haynesville extension is folded into Haynesville, or roughly even with core-only Haynesville) — but Haynesville is unambiguously where this cycle's tracked-family activity concentrated hardest, with Comstock and Expand both pushing deep into the West Haynesville extension counties (Leon, Madison, Freestone, Anderson, Houston) that barely registered in prior cycles. Two "call the client" items are now more overdue than when first flagged: Continental-FireBird has passed its guided close by a full month with still no confirmation found anywhere, including a direct SEC search, and Comstock-SOCAR's October 31 deadline is 23 days out. Three new, large, unidentified lessees — Bull Run Resources (627 leases, West Haynesville), Midcontinent O&G (510, Midland), and Cresta Exploration (217, Midland/Martin) — are each bigger in this file than most of the user's tracked families and warrant a direct name check before the next cycle. The structural caveat underneath everything is unchanged and now chronic: TX permit visibility has been frozen at a 34-day-old snapshot since RRC's IP block took effect, and this pipeline's rig-activity and automated-lease feeds have never worked even once in this repo's history — today's backlog dump is a reminder that "no news" from the daily feeds can mean "data is queued," not "nothing is happening."
