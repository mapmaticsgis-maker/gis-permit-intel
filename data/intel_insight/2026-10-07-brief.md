Giddings corridor is still where the capital is parked. 51 of TX's 95 aging permits sit in the DLS/EOG Giddings & Eastern Eagle Ford counties (Lee, Fayette, Washington, Gonzales, DeWitt). The same Adamas family also holds a separate, much smaller position in the West Haynesville watch corridor — Camp A/Camp B in Robertson County (2 wells, 126 days) — so this is one corporate family running two distinct, geographically separate programs at once.
