GIS ANGLE

The Texas blackout is the opportunity here, not just the obstacle. Ninety-five tracked-family permits are sitting in a confirmed-unspudded state with the state's own data pipe unable to tell us if that's changed — and a meaningful chunk of that book is pad-clustered, not scattered: Firebird's 12 laterals across two Ector pads, Sabine's 10-well combination of Socagee Creek and Bonner North in Panola/Harrison. When the RRC block lifts, these are the pads most likely to show a burst of simultaneous spud activity, and Sabine and Firebird are both already-tracked client relationships in this corridor, not cold outreach. The practical move is to get ahead of that burst now, while the lull holds: refresh plats and land status on the Socagee Creek, Bonner North, Pocket Pair, and Jake P footprints today, rather than waiting for the block to lift and having 20+ locations all need a rushed turnaround at once.
