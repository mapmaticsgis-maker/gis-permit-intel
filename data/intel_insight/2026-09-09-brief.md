GIS ANGLE
The real opening today isn't a new name — it's the five stale TX permits sitting 428-635 days unspudded in Gonzales and Panola (Marathon, EOG, Sabine). Permits that old typically need land and surface-access work re-verified before a rig actually moves — ROW status, plat currency, any lease changes since the original filing — and that re-verification tends to get triggered on short notice once an operator finally sequences the well. Worth a proactive touch to Marathon's and Sabine's land teams now on those specific leases (Cherry Bergey / Roosevelt in Gonzales, Hill CV in Panola) rather than waiting for a spud notice to force a scramble — that's exactly the kind of lead time a standing GIS/plat relationship is supposed to buy.
