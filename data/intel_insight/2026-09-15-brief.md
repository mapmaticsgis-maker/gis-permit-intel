GIS angle: Sabine's 22-permit, up-to-487-day-old East Texas backlog is the standout opportunity here. Permits that sit that long before spud routinely need their original plats, ROW and surface-ownership packages re-verified before drilling actually starts — title chains move, roads get reworked, the as-permitted package goes stale. Getting ahead of that with a proactive plat-refresh offer to Sabine, rather than waiting for a spud notice to trigger the work, is the concrete play. Secondary and smaller: Discovery Natural Resources' North Creek pad in Reagan County has permits filed at well-numbers 401, 402 and 404, then again at 416 and 417 — with 403 and the entire 405–415 range still unfiled. That is not a clean sequential gap; it is two scattered mini-clusters bookending a wide open middle, which reads as a larger multi-well unit still being permitted in stages. Worth a heads-up to land/GIS now, ahead of whatever files next in that block.
