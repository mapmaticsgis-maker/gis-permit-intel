GIS-ANGLE: The real signal today isn't a new name, it's the stale-next-to-active pattern at Marathon (Gonzales) and Sabine (Panola/Harrison). Permits sitting 15-20+ months unspudded next to an operator's live pads mean the original plats, access roads and staking packages for those older locations are due for a refresh before drilling actually starts — spacing rules, adjacent development, and county road conditions can all have shifted since a 2024 or early-2025 filing. Getting ahead of that with updated survey/plat packages for Marathon's Gonzales inventory and Sabine's Panola inventory now, before either operator moves those wells into an active spud queue, is a concrete, low-risk pitch rather than waiting to be asked once they're already mobilizing rigs.
