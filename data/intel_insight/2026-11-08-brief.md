TEXAS: THE QUIET IS THE RRC BLOCK, NOT A LULL

TX's latest issue date on file is still September 4, 2026 — two months stale. That's not operators going quiet; it's the RRC IP block that's been in place since September 17 (confirmed by phone with the agency), which hits both the daf420 MFT feed and the W-1 subscription portal. We're not missing activity because nothing's happening — we're missing it because we can't see it right now. Every TX read below is a snapshot frozen as of early September, and the client-watch aging list is the only thing actually moving: it's aging in place, permit by permit, while we're dark.

And that list has gotten heavy. Ninety-five unspudded permits now sit in tracked-family corridor counties, and three operators account for 59 of them.

GIS ANGLE
